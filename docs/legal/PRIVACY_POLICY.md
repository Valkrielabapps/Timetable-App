# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: [EFFECTIVE_DATE]**

[LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME] ("**we**", "**us**", "**our**") operates [PRODUCT_NAME] (the "**Service**"), a web application that helps schools and colleges generate and manage class timetables. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

## 1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to:
- **School administrators and other staff** who create an account, enter their school's data, and use the Service ("**Account Holders**").
- **Visitors** to our website who have not created an account.

It does **not** directly cover students. We are not designed to collect or store personal information about individual students — see Section 3.

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Information you give us directly
- **Account details**: name, email address, and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text) when you sign up, or your Google account's name/email if you sign in with Google.
- **School data you enter**: the names of subjects, teachers (including teacher email addresses, if you choose to add them, for invite and substitution purposes), rooms, class groups/sections, periods, scheduling rules, and the timetables generated from them.
- **Invitations**: if you invite a colleague to join your school's account, we collect the email address you provide for that invitation.
- **Support communications**: anything you send us directly, e.g. by email.
- **Uploaded files**: if you use bulk import or "extract setup from a document," the spreadsheet file you upload is processed to create records in your account (see Section 4 on how AI processing works).

### 2.2 Information collected automatically
- **Usage and log data**: standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, timestamps) generated by our hosting providers for security and debugging purposes.
- **Authentication tokens**: a session token is stored in your browser's local storage after you log in, so you don't have to log in on every page load.

We do **not** currently use third-party advertising trackers, analytics cookies, or behavioral tracking scripts. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

### 2.3 Information about students
Our data model is deliberately built to avoid needing individual student records. Class groups (sections) store only an aggregate student count (e.g. "32 students in Grade 8 - A"), not names, IDs, or any other individual student information. If your school chooses to enter individually identifying student information into free-text fields anyway, you are responsible for ensuring you have the right to do so under your school's own policies and applicable law; we recommend against it.

## 3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:
- Provide, operate, and maintain the Service, including generating timetables and running the scheduling engine.
- Authenticate you and maintain your session.
- Send transactional emails: account invitations, password reset links, and (once billing is enabled) payment receipts and subscription notices.
- Provide customer support.
- Detect, prevent, and address technical issues, abuse, or security incidents.
- Comply with legal obligations.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use your school's data to train any general-purpose AI model.

## 4. AI features and third-party processing

Certain features — plain-language rule parsing, infeasibility explanations, conversational timetable editing, and "extract setup from a document" — send relevant data (e.g. the rule text you type, the relevant portion of your timetable, or an uploaded spreadsheet's contents) to **Anthropic** (the maker of the Claude AI models) via their API, solely to generate the requested output. Anthropic processes this data under its own API terms and does not use API data to train its models by default. These features can be avoided entirely by using the equivalent manual entry methods (typed rules use a non-AI fallback parser automatically when the AI service is unavailable).

## 5. Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers who help us run the Service, each acting under their own security and privacy commitments:

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Railway | Hosts our backend application |
| Vercel | Hosts our frontend website |
| Supabase | Hosts our production database (PostgreSQL) |
| Anthropic | Powers optional AI features (see Section 4) |
| Resend | Sends transactional emails (invites, password resets) |
| Razorpay | Processes subscription payments, once billing is enabled |
| Google | Provides "Sign in with Google," if you choose to use it |

We do not share your information with data brokers or advertisers. We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or to protect our rights, users, or the public from harm.

## 6. Data retention

We retain your account and school data for as long as your account remains active. If you close your account or your school stops using the Service, we will delete your school's data within a reasonable period upon request, except where we are required to retain certain records (e.g. payment records) for legal or tax purposes.

## 7. Data security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and role-based access control (owner/admin/viewer roles limit who at your school can see or change what). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [SUPPORT_EMAIL]. If you are located in India, these rights are provided consistent with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

You can delete individual records (teachers, subjects, etc.) yourself at any time within the app. To delete your entire account or school, contact us.

## 9. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for use by school administrators and staff, who must be adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. As noted in Section 2.3, our product is designed to avoid storing individual student data.

## 10. International data transfers

Our infrastructure providers (Section 5) may process and store data outside of your country, including in the United States and other jurisdictions. By using the Service, you consent to this transfer.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify Account Holders by email or through a notice on the Service before the changes take effect.
