# Refund and Cancellation Policy

**Effective date: 12 October 2026**

This policy applies to payments made for Timetablz, provided by Arpith David Ratnaraj, sole proprietor, trading as Timetablz ("**we**", "**us**"). It forms part of our Terms of Service.

## 1. What you pay for

Timetablz has a Free plan, which never involves a payment, and a Pro plan at ₹4,999 per year, paid in advance for one year at a time through Cashfree Payments. Business plans are priced and invoiced individually under a separate agreement. All prices are in Indian Rupees (INR).

## 2. Cancelling

You can cancel the Pro plan at any time. To cancel, email **support@timetablz.com** from the email address of a school admin, with the name of your school. The Pro plan does not renew automatically, so there is nothing to switch off: if you simply don't renew, your school moves to the Free plan when the paid year ends and nothing further is charged.

## 3. Pro-rata refunds

If you cancel during a paid year, we refund the unused part of it:

- **Refund = amount paid ÷ 12 × number of full months remaining** after the date we receive your cancellation email.
- Partial months are not refunded.
- Example: you pay ₹4,999 on 1 June and cancel on 15 September. Eight full months remain (October to May), so the refund is ₹4,999 ÷ 12 × 8 = ₹3,332.67.
- When the refund is issued, your school moves to the Free plan. Your timetables and data stay in your account.

## 4. Payments that went wrong

If you were charged twice for the same order, or charged but your plan was not upgraded, write to **support@timetablz.com** with the order reference shown on the payment page. We refund any duplicate or failed payment in full.

## 5. How and when refunds are paid

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method (card, UPI or bank account) through Cashfree Payments. We approve or respond to every request within 3 working days, and refunds usually reach you within 5 to 7 working days of approval, depending on your bank.
