# Refund and Cancellation Policy

**Effective date: 9 November 2026**

This policy applies to payments made for Timetablz, provided by Arpith David Ratnaraj, sole proprietor, trading as Timetablz ("**we**", "**us**"). It forms part of our Terms of Service.

## 1. What you pay for

Every new school starts with a 14-day free trial, which never involves a payment. After that, Timetablz is paid for by plan, per year, in advance, through Cashfree Payments:

| Plan | Sections | Software, per year | Done for you, first year |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | up to 30 | ₹9,999 | ₹17,999 |
| Standard | 31 to 50 | ₹14,999 | ₹26,999 |
| Plus | 51 to 80 | ₹19,999 | ₹34,999 |
| Large / Group | more than 80 | ₹19,999 + ₹250 per section above 80 | quoted |

"Done for you" is the plan's first year plus our team setting the school up (entering its staff, subjects and class plans and making its first timetable); after the first year it renews at the software price. Large / Group plans are priced and invoiced individually. All prices are in Indian Rupees (INR).

## 2. Cancelling

You can cancel at any time. To cancel, email **support@timetablz.com** from the email address of a school admin, with the name of your school. Plans do not renew automatically, so there is nothing to switch off: if you simply don't renew, nothing further is charged, and the school is paused after the 15-day grace period. Your data is kept.

## 3. Pro-rata refunds

If you cancel during a paid year, we refund the unused part of the software:

- **Refund = software price ÷ 12 × number of full months remaining** after the date we receive your cancellation email.
- Partial months are not refunded.
- Example: you pay ₹14,999 for Standard on 1 June and cancel on 15 September. Eight full months remain (October to May), so the refund is ₹14,999 ÷ 12 × 8 = ₹9,999.33.
- **Done for you:** the setup part (the difference between the Done for you price and the software price, for example ₹12,000 on Standard) is refunded in full if you cancel before we have started setting the school up, and is not refunded once we have started. The software part is refunded pro rata as above.
- When the refund is issued, the school's plan ends. Your timetables and data stay in your account.

## 4. Payments that went wrong

If you were charged twice for the same order, or charged but your plan was not applied, write to **support@timetablz.com** with the order reference shown on the payment page. We refund any duplicate or failed payment in full.

## 5. How and when refunds are paid

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method (card, UPI or bank account) through Cashfree Payments. We approve or respond to every request within 3 working days, and refunds usually reach you within 5 to 7 working days of approval, depending on your bank.
